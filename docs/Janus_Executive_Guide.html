Pillar I

Home Systems & Physical Infrastructure

Every system in the residence, controlled from one place — no more juggling vendor apps.

01

One Command Deck for the Entire Estate

Crestron lighting scenes, thermostats, fireplaces, sauna, bath towel warmers, and pool pumps — on a single touch screen.

  • Live status with instant scene activation and setpoint sliders
  • Installs as a native web app on iPhones, iPads, wall touchscreens, and laptops
  • Replaces Crestron, Alarm.com, iAquaLink, Watts, and Govee vendor apps
02

Pool & Spa Hydrology, Simplified

Variable-speed pumps, gas heaters, water features, and spa mode with real-time temperature tracking.

  • One-tap spa heat-up with predictive countdown to target temperature
  • Live 24-hour temperature history chart instead of guessing readiness
  • Integrated directly with Zodiac / iAquaLink without vendor UI latency
03

Interactive Estate Topology & Smart Irrigation

A visual 2D map of every Rain Bird irrigation zone across lawns, groves, and gardens.

  • Visual property layout — tap directly on zones rather than memorizing numbers
  • Automatic weather intelligence suspends watering when rain is forecast
  • Emergency master shutoff valve in case of line breaks or repairs
04

Tesla EV Fleet on the Same Screen

Fleet API integration: battery percentages, charging rates, cabin climate pre-conditioning, and family maps.

  • Lock, unlock, and trigger cabin cooling/heating from the dashboard
  • Live location map tied directly into the family arrival tracker
  • No more switching between separate automotive apps and home controls
